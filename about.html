👋 About Peak Grades

We help students pass their exams and reach their potential.

Peak Grades is a GCSE tutoring service built around one simple idea: every student who wants to improve deserves clear, expert support that actually works.

Our story

Built by a student who's been there.

Peak Grades was started by a UCL Economics and Statistics student who remembers exactly what it felt like to struggle with a topic before it finally clicked.

The tutoring at Peak Grades is built around what actually works: clear explanations before method, structured sessions with a real focus, and honest feedback that helps students improve rather than just feel good.

Since launching, Peak Grades has helped over 50 GCSE Maths students — and 95% improved by at least one grade.

Our mission

Three things we stand for.

Genuinely personalised support

Our approach

Why understanding beats memorising.

Most students who struggle with Maths aren't bad at Maths — they've just been taught to follow steps without knowing why. When you understand the logic, everything becomes easier to remember, apply and adapt in an exam.

That's the core of how Peak Grades works. We don't rush through a method. We make sure it makes sense first. Then the practice follows naturally.

Students who go through this approach don't just get better grades — they stop dreading Maths.
