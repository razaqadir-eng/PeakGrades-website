Notes

Right-angled triangles

SOH CAH TOA: sin θ = opp/hyp, cos θ = adj/hyp, tan θ = opp/adj.

Non-right-angled triangles

Label sides with lowercase letters opposite the matching capital angle.

3D problems

  1. Find a right-angled triangle in the base and use Pythagoras to get a base diagonal.
  2. Make a second right-angled triangle that includes the height.
  3. The angle between a line and a plane uses the line and its projection on the plane.

Bearings

Measured clockwise from North, always with three figures (for example 077°). Draw North lines at each point and use "co-interior angles add to 180°" to relate forward and back bearings.

Common mistakes

  • Calculator in radians instead of degrees. Always check the mode.
  • Using the sine rule when you do not have a matching pair.
  • Rounding too early. Keep full calculator values until the end.
  • For the cosine rule, forgetting to square root at the end.

Exam tips

  • Draw and label the triangle, even when a diagram is given.
  • Ambiguous case: an angle found with the sine rule could be obtuse if it is opposite the larger side.

Worksheet

Total: 25 marks. Calculator allowed except Q5. Give answers to 3 significant figures unless stated.

  1. In triangle ABC, a = 8 cm, b = 11 cm and angle C = 40°. Find the length of side c. (3)
  2. In triangle ABC, angle A = 48°, angle B = 65° and a = 9.2 cm. Find the length of side b. (3)
  3. A triangle has two sides of length 7 cm and 9 cm with an included angle of 58°. Find its area. (2)
  4. A triangle has sides 5 cm, 7 cm and 9 cm. Find the size of the largest angle. (3)
  5. Without a calculator, show that tan 60° − tan 30° = 2/√3. (3)
  6. A cuboid ABCDEFGH has base ABCD with AB = 8 cm, BC = 6 cm and vertical height CG = 5 cm. Find the angle between the diagonal AG and the base ABCD. (4)
  7. A ship sails from port P for 12 km on a bearing of 040° to point Q. It then sails 9 km on a bearing of 130° to point R. Find the distance PR and the bearing of R from P. (5)
  8. Find the exact value of sin 60° × cos 30° + tan 45°. (2)
Show mark scheme and answers (tutor copy)

Try the worksheet first, then check your work here.

  1. c² = 8² + 11² − 2 × 8 × 11 × cos 40° (M1) = 185 − 134.82 = 50.18 (M1). c = 7.08 cm. (A1)
  2. b/sin 65° = 9.2/sin 48° (M1). b = 9.2 × sin 65° / sin 48° (M1) = 11.2 cm. (A1)
  3. Area = ½ × 7 × 9 × sin 58° (M1) = 26.7 cm². (A1)
  4. The largest angle is opposite the longest side (9 cm) (M1). cos θ = (25 + 49 − 81)/(2 × 5 × 7) = −7/70 = −0.1 (M1). θ = 95.7°. (A1)
  5. tan 60° = √3 and tan 30° = 1/√3 = √3/3 (M1). √3 − √3/3 = 2√3/3 (M1) = 2/√3 after multiplying top and bottom by √3 and simplifying (A1).
  6. Base diagonal AC = √(8² + 6²) = 10 cm (M1, A1). tan θ = 5/10 (M1). θ = 26.6°. (A1)
  7. The back bearing from Q to P is 220°. Angle PQR = 220° − 130° = 90° (M1). PR = √(12² + 9²) = 15 km (M1, A1). Angle QPR = tan⁻¹(9/12) = 36.9° (M1), so the bearing of R from P is 40° + 36.9° = 077° (A1).
  8. (√3/2)(√3/2) = 3/4, then 3/4 + 1 = 7/4. (M1, A1)
