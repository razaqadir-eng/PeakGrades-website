FAQ

Course questions.

Are courses one-to-one or in groups?▾
All courses and sessions are one-to-one. This means every session is tailored to you — no time wasted on topics you already understand.
Can I book a course and regular tutoring together?▾
Yes, and many students do exactly that. Regular weekly sessions alongside a focused pre-exam course is the most effective combination.
How do I pay?▾
Payment details are confirmed when you book. Course packages are paid upfront; individual sessions are paid session-by-session. Bank transfer or card accepted.
What if I need to cancel or reschedule?▾
Please give at least 24 hours' notice. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may not be rescheduled. Life happens — just let me know as early as possible.
