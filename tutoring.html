FAQ

Common questions about tutoring.

Do you cover both GCSE and A-Level?▾
Yes — GCSE Maths (Foundation & Higher) and A-Level Maths (Pure, Mechanics & Statistics). Further Maths support is also available. All sessions are tailored to AQA, Edexcel or OCR.
Are lessons online or in person?▾
All lessons are online via Zoom with an interactive whiteboard, so I can tutor students anywhere in the UK. Lessons can be recorded for review if useful.
How long are sessions?▾
Standard sessions are 60 minutes. 90-minute sessions are available for exam booster work — just mention it when you book.
Can I book a trial session first?▾
Absolutely — there's no minimum commitment. Start with a free call, then book a trial session. Many students then move to weekly lessons, but only if it's working.
What platform do you use?▾
Sessions run on Zoom with a Bitpaper interactive whiteboard. All you need is a laptop or tablet, a stable internet connection, and a stylus (optional but helpful).
